Account & Data Deletion Request

Google Play Compliance • Caldim Products

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once processed, your user account, login credentials, authentication tokens, and personal financial data stored in the cloud will be permanently deleted.

How to Delete Your Account

We provide two convenient ways to delete your account and personal data:

📱 Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the CalBudget application installed on your device, you can delete your account instantly:

  1. Open the CalBudget app.
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon in the bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll down to the Account Actions section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the destructive action in the warning dialog by tapping Delete Permanently.

Your account and cloud session will be deleted immediately, and local storage will be sanitized.

🌐 Option 2: Submit a Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit the form below to request account deletion from our support team:

✓ Your request draft has been prepared. Please send the generated email from your registered address to complete verification.

What Data Is Deleted?

Upon processing your deletion request, the following information is permanently purged:

  • User Profile: Name, registered email address, hashed passwords, OAuth tokens, and unique user ID.
  • Session Data: All active authentication tokens and device associations.
  • Sole-Member Household Data: If you are the sole member of a household, all associated entries, categories, planned bills, receivables, sinking funds, budgets, and credit card entries are deleted permanently.

Multi-Member Household Notice

If you belong to a shared household with other active family members:

  • Your personal user account and individual credentials will be deleted.
  • If you were the household owner, ownership is automatically reassigned to the next oldest household member.
  • Shared financial transactions belonging to the household will remain accessible to the remaining household members so their budget remains intact.

Processing Timeline

  • In-App Deletion: Immediate.
  • Web/Email Request: Processed within 7 business days following identity verification.
