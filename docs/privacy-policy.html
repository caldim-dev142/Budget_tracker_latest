Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 • Effective date: September 10, 2026

Summary: CalBudget is built on an offline-first architecture. Your personal finances belong strictly to you. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your transaction data, and we do not display targeted advertisements.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Caldim Products ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use the CalBudget mobile application (the "Application" or "App").

By using CalBudget, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide personal and household financial planning features, CalBudget processes the following categories of data:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in via Google OAuth or Email, we collect your name, email address, and unique user identifier.
  • Financial Records: Incomes, expenses, categories, planned bills, credit card records, sinking funds, saving goals, and household budgets that you manually enter into the app.
  • Device & Authentication Security: Biometric authentication (Fingerprint / Face ID) is processed entirely by your device's native operating system (Android BiometricPrompt / iOS LocalAuthentication). We never access, store, or transmit your raw biometric data.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

  • Offline-First Local Storage: All your financial records are stored in an encrypted local database on your mobile device. You can use CalBudget completely offline without an internet connection.
  • Encrypted Synchronization: When cloud synchronization is enabled, your data is transferred over secure HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections to our PostgreSQL database.
  • Token Security: Authentication tokens (JWTs) and user session credentials are stored in the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain via Flutter Secure Storage.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do never sell, trade, or share your financial transactions, budgets, or personal details with third-party advertisers or brokers.
  • Authentication Services: We use Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication strictly to verify your identity upon login.
  • Legal Compliance: We will only disclose your data if legally required to do so by applicable laws, subpoenas, or judicial orders.

5. Household Budget Isolation

If you create or join a shared Household budget:

  • Financial entries created under the household are shared exclusively with authorized members of that specific household.
  • Users in other households cannot view, access, or modify your household records.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your user account and personal cloud data at any time within the app by navigating to Settings → Account Actions → Delete Account.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: If you do not have access to the app, you can request account deletion online at:
    https://www.caldimproducts.com/calbudget/account-deletion
  • Upon account deletion, your credentials, session tokens, and personal profile are permanently removed from our servers.

7. Children's Privacy

CalBudget is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
